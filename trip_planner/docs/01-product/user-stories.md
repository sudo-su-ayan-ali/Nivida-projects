## Epic 1: Destination & Pilgrimage Intelligence (DISC)

| Story ID | As a... | I want to... | So that... | Priority |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **US-DISC-01** | Devotee / Pilgrim | Browse verified temple cards for Ambaji and Mount Abu with daily Aarti and Darshan schedules | I can arrive at the sanctum at the right time without facing unexpected closures. | P0 |
| **US-DISC-02** | Family Elder | Filter attractions by accessibility (wheelchair friendly, ropeway, elevator, low step count) | I can avoid physical exhaustion and plan appropriate support for elders in my party. | P0 |
| **US-DISC-03** | Traveler | View cultural etiquette guidelines (dress codes, shoe drop stands, photography bans) | I respect sacred temple customs and avoid being turned away at security gates. | P0 |
| **US-DISC-04** | Pilgrim | Access official booking links for trust darshan passes and poojas directly | I am protected from fake third-party ticket scammers and scalpers. | P0 |
| **US-DISC-05** | Vernacular User | Search and view destination information in Hindi or Gujarati | I can navigate the application comfortably in my primary language. | P0 |

## Epic 2: AI-Powered Yatra & Trip Planning (PLAN)

| Story ID | As a... | I want to... | So that... | Priority |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **US-PLAN-01** | Traveler | Enter trip duration (1–5 days), starting point, party makeup, pace, and dietary needs | The platform generates a tailored, day-by-day travel itinerary. | P0 |
| **US-PLAN-02** | Pilgrim | Have the AI itinerary strictly validated against temple opening times and realistic transit | I don't receive impossible schedules that arrive at closed gates. | P0 |
| **US-PLAN-03** | Budget Traveler | View an itemized cost estimate (Stay, Transport, Meals, Activities) | I can plan my travel finances accurately before departure. | P0 |
| **US-PLAN-04** | Traveler | Save my generated itinerary to my local device and cloud profile | I can view my schedule during travel even when mobile network drops. | P0 |
| **US-PLAN-05** | Traveler | Swap or delete activities within my itinerary | I retain complete control to customize my schedule. | P1 |
