## 2. Release Breakdown

### Phase 1: MVP Pilot Release (Mount Abu & Ambaji)
* **Goal**: Validate high-trust discovery, AI itinerary generation, and direct merchant enquiries in a high-density, multi-state pilgrimage circuit.
* **Geographic Coverage**: Mount Abu (Sirohi, Rajasthan), Ambaji (Banaskantha, Gujarat), and Abu Road transit corridor.
* **Core Capabilities**:
  * Rich, verified destination cards with Aarti and Darshan schedules.
  * AI itinerary generation with server-side validation and curated fallbacks.
  * Directory of verified hotels, dharamshalas, licensed guides, and cab drivers with lead dispatch.
  * Dual-language client support (English & Hindi, Gujarati UI copy ready).
  * Offline local caching of saved itineraries and emergency contacts.

### Phase 2: Regional Interstate Expansion
* **Goal**: Expand across the broader Western India tourism and pilgrimage belt.
* **Geographic Coverage**: Somnath, Dwarka, Girnar, Udaipur, Ranakpur, Kumbhalgarh.
* **New Capabilities**:
  * Interactive route maps with road closure and ghat/hilly terrain warnings.
  * Drag-and-drop itinerary reordering.
  * Verified review and moderation pipeline for merchants and attractions.
  * SMS/WhatsApp transactional notification updates for business enquiries.

### Phase 3: Northern Spiritual Corridors
* **Goal**: Expand into the high-traffic spiritual belts of Northern India.
* **Geographic Coverage**: Braj Bhoomi (Mathura, Vrindavan), Varanasi, Ayodhya, Haridwar, Rishikesh.
* **New Capabilities**:
  * Seasonal festival modes (e.g., Holi, Navratri, Shravan yatras) with temporary crowd traffic diversions.
  * Direct in-app payment escrow for verified local guides and homestays.
  * Consensual group tracking and lost-and-found family meeting points.

### Phase 4: Pan-India Tourism & Multi-Modal Transit
* **Goal**: National rollout across all 28 states and 8 union territories.
* **New Capabilities**:
  * Southern circuits (Tirupati, Madurai, Rameshwaram) and Buddhist circuits (Bodh Gaya, Sarnath).
  * Integration with Indian Railways (IRCTC) and state road transport (GSRTC/RSRTC) schedules.
  * Full vernacular support across 8+ Indian regional languages.
