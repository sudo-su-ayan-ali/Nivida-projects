## 2. Redirection & Disclaimer Protocols

When users click an external link to book official pooja or trust dharamshala rooms, the application enforces a mandatory **Trust Redirection Modal**:
> *"You are now redirecting to the official website of [Trust Name]. Bharat Yatra is an independent intelligence platform and does not charge booking fees or issue official temple passes directly."*
