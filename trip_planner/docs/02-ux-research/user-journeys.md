## 1. Journey 1: Devout Family Pilgrimage (Ramesh Patel)

```text
[Home Discovery] ──► [Filter: Ambaji + Elder Support] ──► [Check Aarti & Step Count] ──► [Generate 2-Day Yatra] ──► [Save & Offline Cache]
```

1. **Discovery & Exploration**: Ramesh opens Bharat Yatra in Gujarati. He sees the "Shri Arasuri Ambaji Mata" card highlighted under Pilgrimage circuits.
2. **Reviewing Temple Specifics**: He views the verified Mangla and Sandhya Aarti timings. The accessibility card informs him that Gabbar Hill has a fully operational ropeway (Udankhatola) with lift access for senior citizens, relieving his walking anxiety.
3. **AI Yatra Synthesis**: He inputs a 2-day trip starting from Abu Road station with 4 adults (including 2 seniors) and selects "Relaxed Pace" and "Pure Vegetarian".
4. **Validation & Review**: The platform provides a balanced schedule: Day 1 dedicated to Ambaji Temple Darshan and Mansarovar; Day 2 covering Gabbar Hill and Kumbhariya Jain temples, complete with scheduled rest periods and Satvik Bhojanalaya recommendations.
5. **Execution & Peace of Mind**: He saves the itinerary to his phone. As they travel through rural network dead zones, the itinerary remains fully viewable offline with local emergency numbers.

## 2. Journey 2: Cultural Explorer & Local Taxi Hire (Ananya Sharma)

```text
[Search: Mount Abu Heritage] ──► [Review Dilwara Rules] ──► [Request Taxi Enquiry] ──► [Receive Quote & Confirm] ──► [Experience Trip]
```

1. **Discovery & Rules Check**: Ananya searches for Mount Abu heritage sites. She discovers Dilwara Jain Temples, noting crucial verified details: strict no-photography policy, opening times for tourists (12:00 PM – 5:00 PM), and dress code prerequisites.
2. **AI Trip Customization**: She generates a 3-day itinerary focusing on trekking and architecture. She swaps a shopping stop for Trevor’s Tank nature sanctuary.
3. **Local Cab Hire**: Needing reliable transport from Abu Road to Mount Abu and local sightseeing, she browses verified taxi operators. She selects "Vikram Solanki - Mount Abu Cabs" (verified commercial badge, 4.8-star rating).
4. **Submitting Enquiry**: She submits travel dates, pickup location, and passenger count (solo). Her phone number is masked.
5. **Confirmation**: Vikram accepts the enquiry within 45 minutes and sends a verified quote. Ananya accepts, contact details are unlocked, and pickup is scheduled smoothly.
