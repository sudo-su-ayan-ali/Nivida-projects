## 1. Research Hypotheses & Validation Plan

| ID | Initial Assumption / Hypothesis | Risk Level | Validation Test Method | Success Metric / Gate |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **HYP-01** | Devotees will prefer an AI-generated itinerary over human travel agent recommendations if temple timings are proven authentic. | High | A/B prototype testing with 20 devotee families comparing AI generated vs. agent curated plans. | $\ge 75\%$ express equal or higher confidence in AI plan with verified citations. |
| **HYP-02** | Local taxi drivers in Abu Road will actively respond to digital app enquiries without requiring upfront advance deposits. | High | Pilot onboarding with 10 Abu Road taxi operators using real test leads. | $\ge 80\%$ response rate within 4 hours during daylight operating hours. |
| **HYP-03** | Pilgrims will utilize offline cached itineraries during cellular blind spots (e.g. Guru Shikhar summit or Gabbar foothill). | Medium | Analytics telemetry tracking offline cache access frequency during test trips. | $\ge 40\%$ of saved itinerary views occur in offline/degraded network states. |
| **HYP-04** | A two-way privacy masked phone enquiry model is sufficient to build trust between traveler and business. | Medium | Post-enquiry interviews measuring user comfort with contact reveal timing. | $< 5\%$ dropout at enquiry submission screen due to privacy concerns. |
| **HYP-05** | Senior pilgrims will navigate the UI independently without requiring a younger family member's assistance. | High | Moderated usability testing with adults aged 58+ on localized Gujarati/Hindi builds. | Task completion rate $\ge 85\%$ for finding darshan times and saving a 2-day plan. |
