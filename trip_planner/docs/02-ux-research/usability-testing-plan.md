## 1. Usability Testing Protocol

* **Format**: Moderated usability sessions (remote and in-person field tests in Ahmedabad, Abu Road, and Mount Abu).
* **Participant Pool**: 15 participants across three primary personas:
  * 6 Senior pilgrims / Devotee family heads (Age 55+).
  * 5 Solo and young family holidaymakers (Age 24–40).
  * 4 Local taxi and homestay operators.
* **Testing Devices**: Mid-tier and entry-level Android smartphones (e.g., Redmi Note series, Samsung A-series) and iOS devices.

---

## 2. Core Task Scenarios & Benchmark Metrics

| Task ID | User Goal | Success Benchmark | Max Time Allowed |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **TSK-01** | **Language & Aarti Discovery**: Launch app in Gujarati/Hindi, locate Ambaji Mata Temple, and find the evening Aarti schedule. | Completion rate $\ge 90\%$ | 60 seconds |
| **TSK-02** | **Elder Accessibility Check**: Check if Gabbar Hill has a ropeway or elevator and verify wheelchair access. | Completion rate $\ge 85\%$ | 45 seconds |
| **TSK-03** | **Generate AI Yatra**: Generate a 2-day relaxed itinerary for a family of 4 starting from Abu Road station. | Completion rate $\ge 80\%$ | 90 seconds |
| **TSK-04** | **Save for Offline**: Save the generated itinerary and confirm it remains readable in Airplane Mode. | Completion rate $\ge 95\%$ | 30 seconds |
| **TSK-05** | **Direct Taxi Lead**: Locate a verified Abu Road cab driver and submit an enquiry for 4 passengers. | Completion rate $\ge 85\%$ | 75 seconds |

---

## 3. Evaluative Criteria & Usability Severity Rating

Issues uncovered during testing will be graded using the Nielsen Norman Group severity scale:
* **0 (Cosmetic)**: Minor visual padding or wording issue.
* **1 (Minor)**: User hesitates but completes task without assistance.
* **2 (Major)**: User struggles significantly or experiences confusion; completion delayed.
* **3 (Catastrophic)**: Task failure; user cannot find darshan times or save itinerary.
