## 1. Stakeholder Classification & Roles

| Stakeholder Group | Key Persona / Entities | Primary Needs & Interests | Impact / Influence | Engagement Frequency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **End Users: Travellers & Pilgrims** | Solo travellers, families, senior citizens, devotees visiting Ambaji/Abu. | Accurate temple timings, reliable route planning, accessibility guidance, trusted stays. | High / High | Continuous feedback, UX testing, ratings & reviews. |
| **Local Service Providers** | Mount Abu hoteliers, Ambaji dharamshala managers, taxi operators, local guides. | Genuine customer leads, booking visibility, transparent commission/listing terms. | High / Medium | Onboarding workflows, portal dashboards, dispute channels. |
| **Temple Trusts & Heritage Authorities** | Ambaji Devasthan Trust, Archaeological Survey of India (Dilwara), local civic bodies. | Preserving sanctity, respectful visitor etiquette, crowd dispersal, accurate rules. | High / High | Verification review, official portal links, seasonal festival guidelines. |
| **Engineering & Product Team** | Architects, Flutter devs, Supabase/DB engineers, QA, AI engineers. | Clear requirements, clean architecture, automated testing, maintainable codebase. | High / High | Daily standups, PR reviews, sprint planning. |
| **Platform Operations & Support** | Listing verifiers, content moderators, tier-1 customer support. | Easy admin dashboards, dispute resolution workflows, content publishing controls. | High / Medium | Weekly operations sync, ticket reviews. |
| **Regulatory & Compliance** | Indian Data Protection Board (DPDP Act), consumer affairs bodies. | Privacy compliance, lawful consent, accurate pricing, intermediary liability adherence. | Medium / High | Milestone compliance audits. |
