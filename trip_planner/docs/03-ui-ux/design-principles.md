## 1. Core Design Principles

1. **Sacred Calm & High Legibility**:
   * Pilgrimage and travel experiences should evoke calmness, trust, and clarity—not sensory overload or neon commercialism.
   * Ample breathing room, warm earthy Indian palettes, clear visual hierarchy, and distraction-free typography.
2. **Truth & Transparency over Flashiness**:
   * Timings, pricing, and availability states are presented with unambiguous clarity.
   * Verified badges and official trust portal links are immediately distinguishable from general traveler tips or commercial ads.
3. **Inclusive Across Generations & Devices**:
   * Designed for the 65-year-old grandmother reading Gujarati on a budget Android phone as much as the 25-year-old solo traveler on an iPhone.
   * Minimum touch target of 48×48dp, bold semantic icons, and flexible layouts that gracefully handle dynamic text scaling up to 150%.
4. **Physical Reality & Pacing Empathy**:
   * Visual cues emphasize physical effort: step counts, terrain icons (ghat roads, hill climbs), rest buffers, and weather warnings are prominent in itinerary cards.

## 2. Visual Direction & Aesthetic Identity

* **Theme Concept**: *"Vedic Modernity"* — Blending traditional Indian warmth (terracotta, sacred ochre, marigold, temple stone) with clean, minimalist Swiss-modern UI principles.
* **Iconography**: Clean, 2dp stroke geometric iconography with distinct spiritual and travel badges (Doli/Palanquin, Aarti lamp, Ropeway, Satvik leaf, Heritage pillar).
* **Elevation & Surfaces**: Subtle tonal elevation and soft borders rather than harsh drop shadows. Maintains high contrast under bright Indian sunlight.
