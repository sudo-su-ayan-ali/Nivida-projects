* **Scope**: High-fidelity Figma/Flutter interactive prototype of the Mount Abu & Ambaji circuit across mobile and tablet breakpoints.

---

## 2. Review Findings & Approvals

| Flow Reviewed | Usability / Design Feedback | Resolution / Design Adjustment | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Temple Aarti Timings** | Initially tucked inside a tab; users wanted timings immediately visible on opening the screen. | Moved to a prominent sticky card directly below hero image. | Approved |
| **Elder Accessibility** | "Doli" availability wasn't clear for Guru Shikhar and Gabbar Hill. | Added dedicated "Palanquin / Doli" indicator pill with standard local rate band. | Approved |
| **Enquiry Flow** | Travelers were hesitant to submit inquiries without understanding when their phone number would be shared. | Added transparent micro-copy: *"Your phone number remains masked until the operator accepts your request."* | Approved |
| **Gujarati Typography** | Some header text had line heights that were too tight for Gujarati vowel signs (matras). | Increased `height` factor on `Noto Sans Gujarati` headlines from 1.2 to 1.45. | Approved |
