## 1. Brand Identity & Emotional Resonance

The visual identity of **Bharat Yatra** balances spiritual reverence, cultural majesty, and modern reliability:

* **Spiritual Resonance**: Warm saffron, deep marigold, and temple sandalwood convey sacred authenticity.
* **Geographical Wonder**: Earthy forest greens, lake blues, and desert sand tones represent India's diverse landscapes (from Mount Abu's green hills to Ambaji's marble sanctums).
* **Engineering Trust**: Crisp slate and deep charcoal neutrals convey enterprise stability, safety, and security.

## 2. Imagery Guidelines & Asset Standards

1. **Authenticity First**:
   * Use authentic, un-staged photography representing actual temple architecture, heritage stone carvings, and natural landscapes.
   * Avoid exaggerated AI-generated dreamscapes or generic western stock travel photography.
2. **Respect for Sacred Spaces**:
   * Strictly adhere to temple photography guidelines. Where interior sanctum photos are prohibited (e.g., Dilwara Jain Temples, Ambaji Garbhagriha), use approved exterior shots or respectful architectural line art.
3. **Aspect Ratios & Loading**:
   * Destination Hero Cards: **16:9** aspect ratio.
   * Attraction Thumbnails: **4:3** and **1:1** square ratios.
   * Every image must have a low-saturation placeholder blur and graceful error state with fallback vector art.
